Eligible studies, 2010–2026, including reviews (n = 174). A study counts as Wellcome-funded when its recorded funders name the Wellcome Trust; the Burroughs Wellcome Fund, a separate US foundation, is excluded. Counts describe acknowledged funding, not grant amounts. *2026 is incomplete (searches ran to August 2026), so its bar is hatched.
