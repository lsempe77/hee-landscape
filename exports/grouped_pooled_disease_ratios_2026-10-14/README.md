# Grouped disease ratios with pooled DALYs

Research: current eligible publications from 2010–2026 (2026 partial), naming a level-2 cause and a classified setting: L&MICs n=7,331; HICs n=17,692. A publication counts once per named cause and can enter both settings; research shares can sum above 100%. Burden: GBD 2023-round DALY numbers, all ages, both sexes, summed across 2010–2023 and countries in each setting; cause shares are calculated from these sums, not averages of annual percentages. Research retains the original figure’s 2010–2026 period, which extends beyond the available burden years. Research uses publication-year income; burden uses burden-year income, with the same documented overrides as the KLD analysis. L&MICs includes low-, lower-middle- and upper-middle-income countries. All 22 level-2 causes remain separate, including the three injury causes. Unspecified infection/injury codes are excluded; injury coding may undercount research. Missing income classifications are excluded from setting-specific burden. Matching research to burden is a reference point, not an adequacy target. These ratios use study shares and are not KLD.

Rows are grouped by GBD level 1 and ordered by summed global DALYs across 2010–2023 within each group. This is a grouped display of all 22 level-2 causes, not an aggregation into three dots. Research retains the original figure period. The burden distribution is now pooled across all available years and uses burden-year income, rather than the original 2023-only burden reference. PNG/SVG, exact source values and provenance are saved standalone. No map files were changed.
