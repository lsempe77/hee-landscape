# Standalone updated transition figure

Created 14 October 2026 from the current 41,610-record dataset and today's GBD 2023 burden exports. PNG, SVG and PDF versions use the supplied slide's framing, title, colours, annual lines, 2010/2023 burden markers and 2010–2025 research period. The map was not modified.

Research uses the current title/abstract GBD cause coding, rolled up to the three level-1 classes. A study counts once in each class it names; shares are normalised over these study–class links within year. This is the same computation as the current map's GBD transition figure, rather than the older MeSH-based coding underlying the supplied screenshot. The communicable/maternal/nutritional legend is abbreviated; neonatal conditions are included.

Country setting and publication-year World Bank groups identify LMIC research; burden-year income groups identify LMIC DALYs. The final partial year, 2026, is excluded. Research starts with 146 disease-coded LMIC records in 2010 and ends with 821 in 2025.

The updated 2010 burden shares are 51% NCD, 37% communicable/maternal/neonatal/nutritional and 12% injuries; 2023 shares are 61%, 28% and 11%. Unrounded values and annual research shares are in updated_transition_data.csv. provenance.json records source hashes and definitions. The genuine 3ie logo and gradient assets were taken from the user's existing HSF overview presentation.
