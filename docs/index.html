Which countries are included?

The country views cover the 155 countries with disease-burden and health-spending data (disease burden from the Global Burden of Disease study, 2023). 153 of them have at least one study; Mauritius and Eritrea have none.

Available open access

A study counts as open access when its full text can be read online free of charge, either on the publisher's website or in an open repository, without a subscription or paywall.

Open-access status comes from OpenAlex, looked up using each study's DOI. Studies whose status could not be found were left out, so the percentage is the share of studies with a known open-access status that are open access, not the share of all studies in the map.

This overall figure includes studies with no country stated, as well as single-country, multi-country and regional or global studies. Explorer income-group access rates include each study once within each income group it examines, including multi-country studies, using income classifications at publication. Both numerator and denominator use studies with known access status. The groups can overlap, and studies without an identified income group contribute only to the overall rate, so the overall percentage need not lie between the group percentages.

ⓘ Technical details and methods

The map includes studies that compare the costs and health effects of two or more options, and reviews of such studies. Some records may be wrongly labelled as reviews. AI tools helped sort and classify the studies and can make mistakes; each figure says which studies it includes and where information is missing.

Counts of studies are not funding amounts or measures of research quality. 2026 is incomplete: the searches ran during the year.

Almost all included studies are published in English. This reflects how studies were searched, not the wider literature.
