Health economic evaluations · 2010–2026

HEE evidence map

This project maps the global evidence base of health economic evaluations — studies comparing two or more ways of delivering care on their costs and consequences. From 44,941 records extracted from the harvested literature, 38,199 eligible full evaluations published 2010–2026 are charted here: where they were done, what they studied, how they were built, and whose burden they ignore.
