Act V — Gaps vs Burden (The GBD Climax)

Act V: Gaps vs Burden

The map’s whole point. Variables: study counts × GBD DALYs (country total + 22 Level-2 causes) × income, via crosswalk_mesh_gbd.csv.

C21 — Evidence per Unit of Burden, by Country 
<span class="figure-badge badge-hero">★★★ Hero</span>
<span class="figure-script">R/fig-burden-mismatch.R</span>
Log–log scatter: studies ÷ DALYs × income. The 18× gap between high- and low-income medians.
18× gap in evidence per DALY (HIC median vs LIC median). Within-group spread 50–141× dwarfs between-group 12.9×. The central GBD finding.
C22 — Research Share vs Burden Share, by Disease 
<span class="figure-badge badge-hero">★★★ Hero</span>
<span class="figure-script">R/fig-companion-burden-disease.R</span>
Dumbbell plot: research ÷ burden ratio, LMIC vs HIC, by GBD Level-2 cause.
Maternal-neonatal: 3.6× burden share in HIC vs 0.37× in LMIC. Injuries: 0.21× in LMIC. Cardiovascular, neurological also starkly mismatched. The disease-level gap map.
C23 — Largest Absolute Evidence Deficits 
<span class="figure-badge badge-hero">★★★ Hero</span>
<span class="figure-script">R/fig-companion-deficit.R</span>
Ranked deficit bars: studies vs burden-expected. Where is the MOST missing evidence in absolute terms?
India +4,847 studies short; Nigeria +1,205; Indonesia +920; Pakistan +780; Bangladesh +620. Absolute deficits concentrate in populous LMICs.
C24 — High Burden, Low Research (Injustice Plot) 
<span class="figure-badge badge-hero">★★★ Hero</span>
<span class="figure-script">R/fig-companion-injustice.R</span>
Burden × evidence plane + burden-proportional diagonal, bubble size = population. Which settings have high burden AND low research AND low income?
Editorial closer: bottom-right quadrant = high burden, low evidence, large population, low income. Nigeria, DRC, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Uganda cluster here.
C30 — Within-LMIC Inequality (Lorenz) 
<span class="figure-badge badge-hero">★★★ Hero</span>
<span class="figure-script">R/fig-companion-inequality.R</span>
Lorenz curve of LMIC evidence vs LMIC burden. Is the gap only rich-vs-poor, or steep among the poor too?
Least-served half of LMIC burden holds 16% of evidence; top 5 = 55%. Gini = 0.50. Inequality WITHIN LMICs is extreme — not just HIC vs LIC.
C29 — Epidemiological-Transition Shift 
<span class="figure-badge badge-hero">★★★ Hero</span>
<span class="figure-script">R/fig-companion-transition.R</span>
LMIC research disease-mix (3 transition classes) over 2010–2026 vs 2021 burden. Research shifted communicable→NCD past burden shares; injuries stuck.
Communicable 68%→28%, NCDs 31%→70% (2010→2025). Injuries ~1% throughout vs 11% burden — the transition bypassed injuries entirely.
T4 — Method Streamgraph Over Time 
<span class="figure-badge badge-timeline">Timeline</span>
<span class="figure-script">R/fig-companion-method-stream.R</span>
Econ-eval-type composition over time (2010–2025). Cost-utility 45%→54%, cost-effectiveness 34%→25%.
Field increasingly adopts cost-utility (QALY) methods; cost-effectiveness declining. Method shift mirrors metric shift in C11/T2.
