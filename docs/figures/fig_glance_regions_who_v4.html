Data: 3ie Health Economic Evaluation (HEE) Evidence Map, data as of 14 October 2026; studies published 2010–2026

Regions: the WHO's six regions (AFR Africa, AMR Americas, SEAR South-East Asia, EUR Europe, EMR Eastern Mediterranean, WPR Western Pacific), with Indonesia in the Western Pacific since 2025. Taiwan and Hong Kong, which the WHO does not assign, are placed in the Western Pacific. The United States and Argentina left the WHO in 2026 but stay in the Americas region on the WHO's list. The map covers the 154 countries in the evidence map's country data that have disease-burden data. The country count and trend lines use all 204 countries and territories in the Global Burden of Disease; trends run to 2025.

Disease burden: healthy years lost to illness and early death (DALYs), Global Burden of Disease 2023. Disease focus: diseases coded to Global Burden of Disease 2023 causes from each study's title and abstract, grouped into communicable (with maternal, neonatal and nutritional), non-communicable and injuries. Top three diseases: research topics found by grouping similar abstracts. Utility measure: a QALY or a DALY reported as an outcome, counting economic evaluations only (not reviews). The map and the regional profile count full economic evaluations only (reviews left out); the overview stats count all studies. Rates per 100,000 DALYs divide evaluations published over the whole period by one year's burden (2023). Grey on the map: no economic evaluation or no burden data.
