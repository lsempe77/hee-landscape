Data: HEE evidence map web build (2026-10-08); 2010–2026 publication years.

Regions: WHO Global Health Observatory country list (AFR African, AMR Americas, SEAR South-East Asia, EUR European, EMR Eastern Mediterranean, WPR Western Pacific); Indonesia is in the Western Pacific Region since the World Health Assembly approved its move in May 2025. Places the list does not assign to a region (Taiwan, Hong Kong) get the WHO region of the other countries in their UN M49 sub-region (Eastern Asia: Western Pacific). The United States and Argentina left the WHO in 2026 but the list keeps them in the Region of the Americas, which includes the United States and Canada. Map and trend lines cover the 155 countries in the map's country data; trends run to 2025.

Burden: GBD 2023 all-cause DALYs. Utility measure: QALY reported. Disease groups: 14 harmonised groups from MeSH, grouped into GBD level-1 causes. Top three diseases: research themes from a topic model of the abstracts (team export, 2026-10-11). Map: Robinson projection; grey = no eligible evaluation or no country data.
