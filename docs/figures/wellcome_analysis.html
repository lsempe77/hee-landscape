Climate & Health

How this theme is defined

Reviewed keyword screen: the titles and abstracts of all eligible studies were searched for climate, extreme heat and weather, air pollution, emissions and related terms, and every candidate was reviewed against Wellcome's Climate & Health framing (health impacts of climate change, climate-sensitive infectious disease, air pollution, and mitigation with health co-benefits). Studies of health care's own emissions are included and shown separately. The GBD cause list has no climate category, so this theme does not use disease codes. Wellcome programme scope ↗

Why so few publications? This map covers health economic evaluations (cost-effectiveness and related studies of health interventions) and reviews of them. Most climate-and-health research is epidemiology or impact modelling, so it falls outside the map. Only 102 of 41,610 eligible publications (0.2%) address climate. The 138 candidates found by the keyword screen were each reviewed, and a broader search for related terms found almost no further publications. The small count reflects how thin this evidence is, not a gap in the search.

Technical details and sources

Each funder is counted once per eligible publication. A publication can name multiple funders and belong to multiple themes, so counts do not sum across funders or themes. Funding is recorded for 26.8% of the evidence base, so absence of a name does not establish absence of support. Burroughs Wellcome Fund is a separate organisation and is excluded from the Wellcome cohort.

Setting comparisons use single-country publications with known income, taking the World Bank group in force on 1 January of each publication's year; all other publications stay visible in the multi-country, regional or income-unavailable category. Names are lightly normalised (NIHR, HTA, NIH, MRC, Gates and common variants); agencies and their programmes are not comprehensively merged. Rankings count reported funders, not funding amounts, and tied funders share a rank. A programme named in an acknowledgement does not establish membership in a current priority programme.

Infectious Diseases and Mental Health use the Global Burden of Disease (GBD 2023) causes coded from each publication's title and abstract (the condition the evaluated intervention targets); 36,446 of 41,610 eligible publications have a disease code, so publications without one can still contain relevant evidence. Climate & Health uses a keyword screen of all titles and abstracts (scripts/build_climate_candidates.py), after which every candidate was reviewed against Wellcome's Climate & Health framing; decisions and their basis are in exports/climate_health_review_decisions_2026-10-12.csv. A broader recall check found almost no further publications. Classification uses titles and abstracts, not full texts. The anxiety, depression and psychosis title screen is a separate sensitivity view. Wellcome-funded publications outside all three themes are grouped by keywords in their titles (first matching group wins) to describe what they cover; this grouping does not change any theme count. These definitions do not establish current Wellcome grant eligibility or early-intervention status.
