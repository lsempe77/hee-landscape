How to read these results

Each funder is counted once per eligible study. A study can name multiple funders and belong to multiple themes. Counts do not sum to unique study totals across funders or themes. Funding is recorded for 26.8% of the evidence base, so absence of a name does not establish absence of support.

Income comparisons use single-country studies with known income. All other studies remain visible in the other / unavailable category. Names are lightly normalised (NIHR, HTA, NIH, MRC, Gates and common variants); agencies and their programmes are not comprehensively merged. Rankings are counts of named acknowledgements, not funding amounts. A programme named in an acknowledgement does not establish membership in a current priority programme.

Infectious Diseases and Mental Health use broad existing MeSH-derived disease groups. Climate & Health is a reproducible provisional title screen; full-text validation is outstanding. The focus-condition mental-health title screen is a separate sensitivity view. These definitions do not establish current Wellcome grant eligibility or early-intervention status.

Source: the map's eligible analysis master; web data built 2026-10-08. Publication years 2010–2026 (final year partial). Programme scope checked 12 October 2026: Infectious Disease, Mental Health, Climate and Health.
